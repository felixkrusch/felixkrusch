<p>2026: I shut down RichWP. Then I built the CMS I always wanted.</p>

<p>I shut down RichWP — a WordPress theme business I had built since 2010 — for good.</p>

<p>Yes, I was a die-hard WordPress guy. But it got harder and harder to justify using it.</p>

<p>Pretty much on the same day I had this idea:</p>

<p>What if the CMS was just a conversation?</p>

<p>What if updating your site was as simple as texting someone?</p>

<p>So I built A3lix.</p>

<p>You are a developer.</p>

<p>You have built a gorgeous restaurant website — fast, modern, deployed on Cloudflare Pages.</p>

<p>The client loves it.</p>

<p>Then they ask: "How do I update the menu du jour?"</p>

<p>Now what?</p>

<p>With A3lix, the restaurant owner texts her change to a Telegram bot.</p>

<p>The site updates.</p>

<p>You are not involved.</p>

<p>Everyone is happy.</p>

<p>One message. Done.</p>

<p>No heavy UI. No Gutenberg. No drama.</p>

<p>I launched it just a few days ago with zero marketing.</p>

<p>It already has real traction.</p>

<p>Try it: <a href="https://a3lix.com/">https://a3lix.com/</a></p>
