Your information

Privacy policy.

What information is involved when you visit this website or get in touch, and what I use it for.

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Legal noticePrivacy policyRefer & earn

Who is responsible

Krusch Enterprises Inc., an Alberta corporation, operates this website. Our postal address is 5005 Dalhousie Dr NW Unit 175 #1421, Calgary, AB T3A 5R8, Canada. Contact fk@felixkrusch.com about this policy or your personal information.

When you visit

The site itself does not use analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or cookies. Fonts and images are served with the website, without an external font service or embedded third-party applications.

Preferences such as your selected currency or application view are reflected in the page URL. The website does not save these preferences in cookies or browser storage, and you do not need a visitor account.

The services used to deliver and protect the website process technical request information, which may include your IP address, browser information, requested pages, and the time of access. This information is used to deliver the site, maintain reliability, and help prevent abuse. Service providers may retain technical access and security logs.

When you contact me

If you use the contact form, email, call, or message me, I receive the information you choose to share. The form asks for your email address and message; your name is optional. I use this information to reply, discuss your project, and provide agreed services.

Submitting the form sends your enquiry to my email inbox. The form does not create an account or save your message in a website message database. Email services process the message, and delivered messages are retained in the receiving mailbox.

The form uses spam protection without tracking cookies or a third-party CAPTCHA. Its temporary anti-abuse records do not contain your name, email address, message, or IP address. This does not mean that communication providers, their technical logs, or email mailboxes retain no information.

Email and telephone links open your chosen application. Please do not include passwords or sensitive client records in an initial enquiry.

Use and sharing

Personal information is used to handle enquiries and client relationships, perform agreed work, and meet applicable legal obligations.

Service providers involved in delivering the website and communications may process information as needed to provide those services. They may process it outside your country of residence, where different privacy laws apply. Information may also be disclosed when required by law.

How long information is kept

Enquiries and client correspondence are kept for as long as needed to respond, manage the client relationship, and meet applicable record-keeping obligations. Service providers may have separate retention periods for technical logs and backups.

You can contact me about the information held about you or request its deletion. Legal and record-keeping obligations may affect what can be deleted and when.

Security and your requests

I take reasonable precautions to protect personal information. No method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is completely secure.

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or raise a privacy concern. Email fk@felixkrusch.com or call +1 581 222 2515.

External websites

Project links, social profiles, and WhatsApp links take you to third-party websites or applications. Their own privacy policies apply. Product screenshots on this website are images, not live connections to those applications.

Changes to this policy

This policy will be updated if the information collected, its use, or the website's privacy practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest revision.
