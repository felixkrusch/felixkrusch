A few straight
answers.

Do I need a website or a custom application?

If the job is to explain your business and bring in enquiries, start with a website. If customers need to book, exchange documents, manage accounts, or work with your data, we should talk about an application. Sometimes the right answer is an existing tool. I will tell you that, too.

Can you give me a fixed price?

Yes, once the scope is clear. For the Modern Performance Website, a short call is usually enough to confirm scope and price. Applications need a closer look at users, business rules, data, and integrations before I can quote responsibly. Substantial projects may start with a separately priced definition phase.

What if I need more than the website package?

The Modern Performance Website package includes design, development, technical setup, and a CMS where needed. The CMS is custom-built or open-source, chosen to suit your editing needs and hosting infrastructure. An extra language is EUR 500. Hosting is not included. Additional pages, e-commerce, special integrations, and custom applications are scoped separately.

What happens after launch?

Hosting is not included in the build price. Technical setup is included; hosting, updates, maintenance, and ongoing support responsibilities are agreed explicitly. Continued development is available with its own clear scope.
