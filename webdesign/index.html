What does the starting price cover?

The Modern Performance Website starts at CAD 4,000 / USD 3,000 / EUR 2,500. That includes design, programming, technical setup, on-site SEO, and a CMS where needed. The CMS is custom-built or open-source, chosen to suit your editing needs and hosting infrastructure. We confirm the page count, content, and features together. Hosting is not included. An extra language is EUR 500. E-commerce, custom applications, and special integrations are scoped separately.

How long does a website take?

The usual expectation for the defined website package is 1-3 weeks once the scope, content, and assets are ready. The homepage approval is a real checkpoint. Slow feedback, missing content, or new requirements change the schedule. Larger projects get their own delivery plan.

How do payment and revisions work?

Usually 50% after the design is approved and 50% at launch. Unlimited design revisions are included until you are happy with the result. I build the homepage first so you can approve the direction before I complete the remaining pages. The proposal confirms the payment arrangement. Additional pages or functionality are separate from design revisions and are quoted before I build them.

Can you replace my existing website?

Yes. I review the current pages, content, media, URLs, and features before recommending what to keep or replace. A migration should not casually discard useful content or established URLs. If you need to manage content yourself, a suitable CMS is included in the website package.

Do I need to write a technical brief?

No. Your current URL, a few goals, your logo, photos you have the right to use, and a rough draft of the content are a useful start. We establish the requirements together. New copywriting, branding, photography, and substantial content migration need to be included explicitly if required.

What about SEO and AI search?

The foundation is a clear, fast, crawlable website with accurate metadata, sensible structure, and useful content. That is real work, not an AI ranking package. No responsible provider can promise that Google or an AI assistant will recommend you. Ongoing content or search work is a separate scope.
